UC Berkeley MIDS · Experiment Design · A/B Testing

LLM Prompt Consistency Experiment

  • Within-subjects factorial experiment: 51 prompts, each run under 1 control and 5 treatments × 3 LLMs (ChatGPT, Gemini, Gemma) × 2 temperatures (0, 2) × 10 repeated generations, with consistency measured as average pairwise cosine similarity using sentence embeddings (all-mpnet-base-v2); a parallel image-description experiment was run the same way
  • Pre-registered a power analysis (targeting ~0.94 power at a conservative 4% effect size) to set the sample size, then estimated Average Treatment Effects via regression with condition × temperature and condition × prompt-type interaction terms and cluster-robust standard errors, pre-committing to the full interaction model to avoid p-hacking
  • Temperature, not prompt wording, was the dominant lever. Dropping to temperature 0 drove consistency gains up to +100% in cosine similarity, while 4 of the 5 prompt treatments decreased consistency and no single treatment behaved consistently across all three LLMs

UC Berkeley MIDS · Recommender Systems

Movie Recommender: Matrix Factorization vs. Item-Item CF

  • Built and benchmarked 5 recommenders (popularity baseline, hand-implemented Funk SVD, Item-Item CF, SVD+Bayesian ensemble, tuned Surprise SVD) on MovieLens 1M
  • Best model beat the popularity baseline by 45% on Precision@10 (0.0239 vs. 0.0165) and 39% on HitRate@10 (0.1775 vs. 0.1280), with a test RMSE of 0.879
  • Surfaced a fairness gap through subgroup analysis (younger and male users received measurably better recommendations) and proposed reweighting to address it before deployment

UC Berkeley MIDS · Applied Econometrics

Firm Characteristics & Stock Price Event Study

  • Built a regression model (adjusted R² = 0.996) linking firm-level financial and ESG characteristics to two-week stock price changes for Fortune 1000 firms after a geopolitical shock
  • Found sector membership alone explains ~18% of return variation; Energy-sector firms saw a statistically significant 14.5% higher stock price than Consumer Discretionary firms (p < 0.01), consistent with independent industry reporting
  • Validated findings with cluster-robust standard errors and a full model-assumption battery (collinearity, homoskedasticity, error distribution) across baseline, financial, and ESG specifications
